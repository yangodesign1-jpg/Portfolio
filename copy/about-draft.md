## 2. How it works

A. Conversation / שיחה: we talk about what it's for, who will see it, and what you already have.
B. Idea and sketches / רעיון וסקיצות: a few quick directions on paper, before anything is polished.
C. Drawing and design / ציור ועיצוב: the chosen direction, drawn and built properly.
D. Production / הפקה: files ready for print, screen or wall, and help with the printer if you need it.
E. Handover / מסירה: everything organised, with the sources, so it keeps working after we finish.
